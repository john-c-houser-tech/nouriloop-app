# Supplements count toward intake, but only on days with a meal logged

Every screen that adds up a day's intake (Trends, the Dashboard, the inflammation score, supplement recommendations, Predict, nutrition goals, the GLP-1 protein card, the digests) counts the user's active supplements on top of their logged meals, so a protein shake shows up in the protein goal exactly as it does on the Trends chart. A day with no meal logged has no intake at all, supplements included: an empty day means the user didn't log, not that they lived on capsules, and counting supplements alone would turn every unlogged day into a near-empty "intake" that drags averages and goal check-ins. Decided by John on 2026-10-06, replacing four different per-screen rules (goals and the GLP-1 card ignored supplements; the Dashboard counted them even on empty days).

A supplement counts only from the local day it was added, not on earlier days: otherwise adding a supplement today would lift last week's goal check-ins and redraw past Trends charts overnight (John, 2026-10-07). Switching a supplement off still removes it from past days, because supplements keep no stop history; that needs a real start/stop record if it ever matters.
