# NouriLoop — Feature Overview

> **Status: ACTIVE** *(stamp added 2026-07-12, docs-sync audit — see
> the private business folder's `DOCS-INDEX.md`)*

**NouriLoop turns everyday photos of your food into a clear picture of how you eat — and connects it to how you feel.** Snap a meal, and AI does the tedious part: estimating nutrition, spotting ingredients, and asking the right questions. Over time, NouriLoop closes the loop between your diet, your health metrics, and your symptoms, so you can see what's actually working.

> NouriLoop is an educational wellness tool, not a medical device. Its scores and insights are designed to help you understand patterns in your own data — they are not medical advice or diagnosis.

---

## 📸 Effortless food logging

- **Snap-a-photo nutrition** — Take a picture of your meal and NouriLoop's AI estimates calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, and a full panel of vitamins and minerals.
- **Confirm & Refine** — Every estimate gets a quick review screen where you can adjust ingredients, answer a clarifying question or two, and fine-tune before saving.
- **Portion control** — Ate half? Ate double? Set how many servings you actually had and every number scales instantly.
- **Add a label for accuracy** — Attach a second photo, like a nutrition-facts label, and the AI combines both for a sharper estimate.
- **Log on your schedule** — Forgot to log at the table? Backdate a meal to when you actually ate it.
- **Favorites** — Save the meals you eat often and re-log them in one tap — no re-analysis, no waiting.

## 📊 Understand your nutrition

- **Daily dashboard** — See today's intake and key signals at a glance.
- **Trends over time** — Visual charts of your nutrition and health metrics so you can spot the direction you're heading.
- **Nutrient adequacy & supplement suggestions** — NouriLoop reviews your last week of eating against recommended intakes and flags where you're consistently coming up short.
- **Supplement tracking** — Scan a supplement label with your camera, and NouriLoop reads the facts panel and folds your daily supplements into its adequacy picture — so suggestions reflect what you already take.

## 🔄 Close the loop: diet ↔ how you feel

- **Dietary inflammation score** — A daily, easy-to-read signal of how pro- or anti-inflammatory your food choices were, based on established nutrition research.
- **Symptom check-ins** — Track the symptoms that matter to you (joint pain, digestion, headaches, energy, mental clarity, or your own custom ones) and NouriLoop correlates them with your diet — including next-day effects.
- **Glucose response insights** — If you use a continuous glucose monitor, NouriLoop maps each meal's blood-sugar response, compares it to what your meal's nutrition predicted, and ranks which meals spiked you most and least. A guided **Connect your CGM** setup walks you through linking an Abbott Lingo or Dexcom Stelo sensor step by step, with a live checklist confirming your data is actually flowing.
- **AI Pattern Coach** *(premium)* — Goes a step further: an AI deep-dive reads your own glucose responses across meals — the foods, timing, and combinations behind them — and writes a personalized coaching summary, calling out the patterns it sees, the likely causes, and a few concrete adjustments to try. Grounded entirely in your data.

## 🎯 Personalized goals & coaching

- **Conversational goal setup** — Tell NouriLoop something like "I want to eat more fiber," and an AI coach turns it into a concrete, measurable goal grounded in your own eating habits.
- **Scheduled check-ins** — NouriLoop evaluates your progress on a schedule you choose and delivers a friendly summary, chart, or report straight to your inbox.
- **Safe, realistic targets** — Goals are sanity-checked so you're never nudged toward unsafe restriction.
- **"What if" predictions** *(premium)* — Ask what would happen if you ate a favorite meal daily, added or dropped a supplement, or made some other change — NouriLoop projects the effect on your nutrition, inflammation, and weight using your own data, before you commit to trying it.

## 🩺 Your health data, your way

- **Rich health metrics** — Track weight, blood glucose, blood pressure, heart rate, body fat, steps, and sleep stages alongside your nutrition.
- **Easy imports** — Bring in history from Apple Health, popular health-export apps, or a simple spreadsheet.
- **Automatic syncing** — Connect an automated export so your phone sends new readings to NouriLoop on its own — no manual uploads.

## 🔔 Built for real life

- **Smart reminders** — Optional nudges when you haven't logged a meal, your health readings are getting stale, your calories look off, or your nutrient gaps are widening.
- **Email or text** — Get notifications and goal check-ins however you prefer.
- **Your timezone, your day** — "Today" always means *your* local day, wherever you are.
- **GLP-1 mode** — A dedicated mode for people on GLP-1 medications, with protein-forward guidance, portion help, and awareness of common nausea triggers.
- **Ask NouriLoop** — A built-in help assistant that understands NouriLoop's own features and answers your questions in plain language, right from the Help Center.

## 🔒 Private & secure

- **Personal, multi-user accounts** — Your data is yours, kept separate and secure.
- **Open sign-up** — Anyone can create a free account; a beta-tester code unlocks a complimentary, time-limited Loop+ for early supporters.
- **Delete anytime** — Wipe just your logged data and start fresh, or schedule full account deletion with a 30-day grace period to change your mind.
- **Clear privacy practices** — Plain-language notices explain how your health data is handled, including any services involved in processing it.
